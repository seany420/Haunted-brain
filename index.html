FOR EDUCATION ONLY. The haunted-house language is a teaching metaphor. Real neurobiology is probabilistic and distributed, findings are averages across groups, and no single brain scan diagnoses anyone. Nothing here replaces a clinician. Doses, interactions, and choices belong to a prescriber who knows the person.
